Working well in Intelligent Design

Design to serve

  1. Listen to someone

    Ask about a real difficulty. Describe who your design will help and what a successful solution would do.

  2. Sketch and model

    Explore several ideas, label dimensions, and build a simple CAD model of the most promising solution.

  3. Test and improve

    Try a prototype, gather feedback, make a change, and explain how it better meets the need.

Before requesting a 3D print

Check your dimensions, look for disconnected parts, and consider how the object will be used. Show your model and intended purpose to Mr. Barents before printing.
