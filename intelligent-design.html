Design to serve

  1. Listen to someone

    Ask about a real difficulty. Describe who your design will help and what a successful solution would do.

  2. Sketch and model

    Explore several ideas, label dimensions, and build a simple CAD model of the most promising solution.

  3. Test and improve

    Try a prototype, gather feedback, make a change, and explain how it better meets the need.

Before requesting a 3D print

Check your dimensions, look for disconnected parts, and consider how the object will be used. Show your model and intended purpose to Mr. Barents before printing.

Course Overview

Created to Create

Students explore the engineering design process, practice freehand sketching, learn the basics of 3D design, and create prototypes using 3D printing. The semester culminates in a service-focused design project where each student identifies a real need and develops a solution for someone else.

What Students Learn

Core Skills

💡

Engineering Design

Identify needs, define constraints, brainstorm ideas, build prototypes, test solutions, and improve designs through iteration.

✏️

Sketching

Use freehand sketches, labels, dimensions, and multiple views to clearly communicate design ideas before building.

🧊

3D Design

Learn foundational CAD skills by creating simple digital models and preparing designs for fabrication.

🖨️

3D Printing

Turn digital designs into physical prototypes while learning about print orientation, supports, tolerances, and iteration.

Student Experience

Design to Serve

🃏

Design Challenges

Short build challenges help students practice creativity, teamwork, constraints, testing, and redesign.

📐

CAD & Prototyping

Students progress from sketches to digital models and printed prototypes, learning how ideas become real objects.

🤝

Service Design Project

Each student identifies a real need, designs and builds a solution, and presents how the final design serves another person.
