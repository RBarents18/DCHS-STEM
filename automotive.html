Working well in Automotive

Prepare for a supervised lab

  1. Identify the vehicle

    Record the year, make, model, engine, and mileage. Locate the owner’s manual and applicable service information.

  2. Confirm the procedure

    Review the task, required tools, specifications, and safety setup with Mr. Barents before beginning.

  3. Record and verify

    Document observations and work completed. Have Mr. Barents verify the procedure before returning the vehicle to use.

Maintenance skills to practice

Find the assigned maintenance checklist in Google Classroom for demonstrations and teacher signatures. Connect each task to the correct vehicle specifications, explain what you observed, and distinguish a symptom from a confirmed cause.
