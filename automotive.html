Prepare for a supervised lab

  1. Identify the vehicle

    Record the year, make, model, engine, and mileage. Locate the owner’s manual and applicable service information.

  2. Confirm the procedure

    Review the task, required tools, specifications, and safety setup with Mr. Barents before beginning.

  3. Record and verify

    Document observations and work completed. Have Mr. Barents verify the procedure before returning the vehicle to use.

Maintenance skills to practice

Use the assigned teacher checklist for demonstrations and signatures. Connect each task to the correct vehicle specifications, explain what you observed, and distinguish a symptom from a confirmed cause.

Course Overview

Practical Skills for Real Vehicles

Automotive gives students the confidence to understand and care for a vehicle. Students learn safe shop practices, basic maintenance, vehicle systems, and diagnostic thinking through demonstrations and hands-on work with real tools and components.

What Students Learn

Core Skills

🦺

Shop Safety

Use lifts, jacks, stands, hand tools, power tools, PPE, and safe work procedures responsibly.

🛢️

Routine Maintenance

Check and service engine oil, fluids, filters, tires, batteries, and other common maintenance items.

⚙️

Vehicle Systems

Explore engines, drivetrains, brakes, steering, suspension, charging, starting, and electrical fundamentals.

🔎

Diagnostics

Learn to observe symptoms, gather evidence, test likely causes, and make informed repair decisions.

Student Experience

Hands-On Tasks

🔋

Battery & Starting Systems

Students inspect batteries, understand charging and starting systems, and practice safe jump-start procedures.

🛞

Wheel & Tire Service

Students inspect tires, remove and install wheels, and correctly torque lug nuts using proper shop procedure.

🔧

Maintenance Verification

Students demonstrate essential maintenance procedures for teacher verification, emphasizing both competence and safety.
