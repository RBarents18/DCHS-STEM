Keep an engineering notebook

  1. Define the problem

    Identify the need, measurable success criteria, and constraints before choosing a solution.

  2. Document the evidence

    Save labeled sketches, calculations with units, design versions, test conditions, and raw data.

  3. Justify the redesign

    Compare results with your criteria. Explain what you changed, why, and what you will test next.

Before a project presentation

Show the problem, your design decisions, test results, and the evidence behind your conclusions. Credit sources and contributions, describe limitations, and explain how your design could improve.

Program Overview

Engineering 102 (ENGR 102) is an exciting dual enrollment opportunity that allows Desert Christian high school students to earn University of Arizona college credits while still in high school. This program provides a hands-on introduction to engineering principles and practices.

🎓

Dual Enrollment

Earn University of Arizona college credits while completing high school coursework, giving you a head start on your college education.

🔧

Hands-On Learning

Engage in project-based learning with real-world engineering challenges that develop critical thinking and problem-solving skills.

🤝

Team Collaboration

Work alongside peers in collaborative engineering projects, developing communication and teamwork skills essential for STEM careers.

🚀

Career Preparation

Explore various engineering disciplines and gain valuable experience that prepares you for college and future STEM careers.

Course Details

Course Credit

Earn 3 University of Arizona credits (ENGR 102) that can be applied toward your future college degree.

Curriculum Focus

Introduction to engineering design process, technical communication, teamwork, and hands-on project development.

Prerequisites

Open to motivated high school students with an interest in engineering. Strong math and science background recommended.

Program Duration

Full academic year program with semester-based projects and assessments aligned with university standards.

Benefits of Dual Enrollment

💰

Cost Savings

Significantly reduce college tuition costs by earning credits at a fraction of traditional college prices.

📚

College Readiness

Experience college-level coursework and expectations in a supportive high school environment.

🎯

Competitive Advantage

Stand out in college applications with demonstrated ability to succeed in university-level engineering courses.

University of Arizona Partnership

This program is offered in partnership with the University of Arizona College of Engineering's K-12 Outreach program.
