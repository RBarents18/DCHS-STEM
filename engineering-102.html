Is this course for you?

For students who enjoy building, testing, teamwork, and applying math and science to real problems. This is a full academic-year course.

University-credit eligibility: UA lists enrollment in, or completion with a C or better of, a fourth-year math course at college algebra level or higher. Ask Mr. Barents about DCS placement and UA registration. Read UA requirements. Eligible students earn 3 UA credits upon successful completion; degree applicability depends on the receiving program.

Working well in Engineering 102 HS

Keep an engineering notebook

  1. Define the problem

    Identify the need, measurable success criteria, and constraints before choosing a solution.

  2. Document the evidence

    Save labeled sketches, calculations with units, design versions, test conditions, and raw data.

  3. Justify the redesign

    Compare results with your criteria. Explain what you changed, why, and what you will test next.

Before a project presentation

Show the problem, your design decisions, test results, and the evidence behind your conclusions. Credit sources and contributions, describe limitations, and explain how your design could improve.
