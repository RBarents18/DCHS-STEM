Build, test, explain

  1. Plan the behavior

    State the purpose of your program and what the user will enter and see.

  2. Test deliberately

    Try typical inputs, boundary cases, and unexpected inputs. Record expected and actual results.

  3. Explain your code

    Trace how data changes and explain what each procedure contributes to the program.

Course Overview

Understanding the Digital World

AP Computer Science Principles gives students a broad foundation in computing. Students create programs, analyze data, study how the internet works, examine cybersecurity, and consider how computing innovations affect people and society.

"The simple believe anything, but the prudent give thought to their steps."

What Students Learn

Core Topics

⌨️

Programming

Build interactive apps using variables, conditionals, loops, procedures, lists, and event-driven programming.

📊

Data

Collect, visualize, and interpret data while considering patterns, bias, privacy, and the limits of data-driven conclusions.

🌐

Internet & Networks

Explore binary information, protocols, routing, fault tolerance, and the infrastructure that makes the internet work.

🔐

Cybersecurity & Impact

Examine encryption, security, privacy, digital ethics, and the broader social consequences of computing innovations.

Student Experience

Projects & Practice

📱

App Design

Students plan, build, test, and refine apps while learning to communicate program purpose and design decisions.

🧮

Data Investigations

Students use authentic datasets to identify trends, make claims, and discuss uncertainty and responsible interpretation.

📝

AP Create Task & Exam Prep

Students prepare for the AP Create Performance Task and practice the concepts and reasoning assessed on the AP exam.
