Practice with understanding

  1. Before looking at a solution

    Try the problem yourself. Sketch, identify the relevant concept, and write a first step.

  2. Explain the result

    Show your reasoning, include units, and connect the answer to the graph or situation.

  3. Learn from a mistake

    Find the first incorrect step, correct it, and try a similar problem without the solution.

Project preparation

For Calculus Coaster, label each piece and its domain, check continuity and differentiability at joins, and use derivatives to justify your analysis. For River Flow Analysis, define the rate and time units before interpreting an integral as accumulated volume.

Course Overview

Calculus with Purpose

AP Calculus AB develops conceptual understanding and procedural fluency in limits, derivatives, integrals, differential equations, and applications of calculus. Students learn to explain reasoning, interpret multiple representations, and use mathematics to model real situations.
