About Our Program

Learning with Purpose

The Desert Christian STEM Program equips students to think critically, solve meaningful problems, and use technology with wisdom. Across every course, students are challenged to pursue excellence, work collaboratively, and apply their God-given abilities in service to others.

Courses This Semester

Our current course offerings span advanced mathematics, computer science, engineering, automotive technology, and junior-high design.

∫

AP Calculus AB

Students develop a deep understanding of limits, derivatives, integrals, differential equations, and mathematical modeling while preparing for the AP Calculus AB exam.

💻

AP Computer Science Principles

Students explore programming, data, the internet, cybersecurity, algorithms, and the impact of computing while developing solutions through collaborative, project-based work.

⚙️

Engineering 102 HS

In partnership with the University of Arizona, students apply the engineering design process, teamwork, data analysis, prototyping, and technical communication in a dual-enrollment course.

Explore Engineering 102 →
🔧

Automotive

A practical introduction to vehicle systems, maintenance, shop safety, diagnostics, and hands-on mechanical skills designed to build confidence and real-world automotive knowledge.

✏️

Intelligent Design

Junior-high students learn the engineering design process, freehand sketching, 3D design, prototyping, and 3D printing, culminating in a service-focused design challenge.

Student Work

Hands-On Learning

Students move beyond theory by designing, building, testing, analyzing, and improving real solutions.

🏗️

Engineering Design

Students work through authentic engineering problems using design constraints, modeling, prototyping, experimentation, and technical communication.

Learn about Engineering 102 →
🧰

Automotive Maintenance

Students practice essential vehicle-maintenance skills, including fluids, batteries, tires, brakes, and safe shop procedures using real tools and vehicles.

🖨️

Design to Serve

Intelligent Design students identify a real need, develop ideas, create 3D models, build prototypes, and present a solution designed to serve someone else.
